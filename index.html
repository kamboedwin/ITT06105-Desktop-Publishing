How to study

Build your skills gradually

Start each lecture by reading the explanations and looking at the illustrations. Open the presentation to review examples. Complete the short practical task, answer the review questions and finish with a practice quiz. You can revisit any lecture at any time.

About the quizzes: Each attempt draws eight questions from a larger pool. Your score and corrections appear at the end. These quizzes are for practice; they are not university assessments.

The Tumaini Community Learning Centre is a fictional example used throughout the practicals. Its posters, icons and hand-outs let you apply the same skills across different publishing applications.
